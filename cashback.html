How cashback works

  • Click a store, shop as normal. The store pays us a commission on your order.
  • We share 50% of that commission with you as coins — 1,000 coins = $1.
  • Cashback appears as "pending" first, then becomes available once the store confirms the order (usually 30–60 days, no returns).
  • Never buy something just for the cashback — it only makes sense on things you were buying anyway.
