About PlayGamesEarnMoney

PlayGamesEarnMoney is a get-paid-to (GPT) site. Advertisers pay us when you play their game to a level, try their app, or finish a survey. You keep 60% of that value as coins; we keep 40% to run the site. 1,000 coins = $1. Cash out from $10 via PayPal or $3 in crypto.

What we promise — and what we don't

We promise: real offers from real advertisers, coins that credit when the advertiser confirms, withdrawals reviewed by hand and paid in batches every Tuesday and Friday, and a payout-proof wall that only ever shows real payouts.

We don't promise: fast riches. Nobody gets rich from GPT sites. A casual user earns a few dollars a month; a regular game grinder earns more. If a site promises you $500 a day, it's lying — and app stores have pulled sites for exactly that kind of claim.

Why payouts are gradual

Advertisers pay us 30–60 days after you finish an offer, but you'd expect your money in days. So we pay in batches, cap daily payouts, and hold first withdrawals for a fraud check. That keeps the site solvent — and keeps paying — instead of collapsing in a wave of first-week withdrawals.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site (offer walls, survey routers, cashback stores) are affiliate or partner links: we earn a commission when you complete an offer or shop. That commission funds your coins. We disclose it here and on the relevant pages, as the FTC requires.
