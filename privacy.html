Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. What we collect

Account: your email address and password (hashed by our auth provider, Supabase). Activity: offers you click and complete, coins earned, withdrawals requested, referral code used, daily check-ins. Fraud prevention: a hash of your device fingerprint and of your IP address (we store hashes, not raw values), and your country (from your IP) to show the right offers. Payouts: the PayPal email or crypto wallet address you enter when withdrawing. Support: anything you send us.

2. Why we collect it

To run the service: accounts, coin balances, withdrawals, referrals. To prevent fraud: one account per person/device, bot detection, payout review. To comply with the law: tax reporting for large earners (see Terms §8).

3. Third parties

Offerwalls (Offerwall.GG, AdGem) and survey routers (CPX Research, TheoremReach) receive only your user ID — never your email or name — and track your offer completions to credit you. Ad networks (Infolinks, Monetag, Adsterra and others listed on our pages) may set cookies to serve ads; we never pay you for viewing or clicking ads. Payment processors (PayPal, crypto networks) receive only the payout details you enter. Supabase hosts our database and authentication. Each third party has its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies

We use cookies for login sessions, preferences (like dismissing the push notification prompt), and advertising. You can block cookies in your browser, but login will not work without session cookies. Ad cookies can be opted out via your browser or the network's opt-out page.

5. Children

This site is 18+ only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights & deletion

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction of wrong data, or deletion of your account (email support@playgamesearnmoney.com). Deleting your account forfeits any unwithdrawn coins. We keep payout records as required for tax and fraud prevention. If you are in California or the EU/UK, you have the additional rights those laws grant (access, deletion, opt-out of sale/sharing — we do not sell your data).

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Contact: support@playgamesearnmoney.com.
