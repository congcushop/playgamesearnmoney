Questions?

Is it really free?

Yes. Joining is free and withdrawing is free — we never charge you to cash out. We earn a share from advertisers when you finish an offer; you keep 60% of that value in coins.

How fast do I get paid?

Withdrawals are reviewed by hand and paid in batches every Tuesday and Friday. Your first withdrawal is held up to 48 hours for a fraud check. After that, batches are the normal rhythm.

Why do game offers take days to credit?

Can I have more than one account?

No — one account per person, one per device. Extra accounts are banned and their balances are forfeited. Use your referral link to invite friends instead.
